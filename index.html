    Postcard

    The look of the Parks Tour: warm paper, soft pastel fills, deep slate ink and one terracotta accent. Things are rounded, they lean a little, and they press down when you touch them. Plain CSS and a few vanilla helpers, ready to carry into any framework.

    • 3 themes
    • 2 typefaces
    • AAA text contrast
    • 0 dependencies
    • 1 accent

    Principles

    What makes it feel like a postcard, and what keeps it easy to read.

    • ♥

      One strong colour

      Terracotta marks the thing to do next and where you are. Everything else is paper, ink and soft pastels.

    • ↻

      Things lean

      Stamps sit at −4°, cards wobble when you reach for them, the mark tips its hat. A little tilt, used in a few places.

    • ↓

      Pressable

      Buttons and pills sit on a short hard shadow and sink into it when pressed, like a key.

    • Aa

      Readable first

      Body and muted text reach 7:1 contrast in every theme. Targets are at least 44px. Motion stops when you ask it to.

    Colour

    Every colour is a token. Switch to Dusk at the top to see them all change together.

    Surfaces and ink

      Pastels, each with its ink

        Each pastel has an -ink that reaches 7:1 on it. They double as meanings: success is sage, info sky, warning butter and danger rose.

        A colour per day

        dayColor(i, total) spreads items round the hue wheel from terracotta through sage and sky to lilac. Set the result as --pc-c and stamps, cards, tags and the rail tint themselves from it.

        Type

        Fraunces at its softest, with a touch of wonk, for headings. Figtree for everything else.

        Fraunces · SOFT 100 · WONK 1

        Wild & wonky

        Rounded terminals and a few wayward letters. Use it for titles and numbers on stamps, never for long text.

        Figtree · 400 to 750

        Plain, friendly, clear.

        For reading and for controls. Weights: 400 for text, 600 for labels, 650 for buttons, 750 for small capitals.

              Accessibility

              Built in, so each app doesn't have to remember it.

              • Skip link

                Press Tab on this page: a "Skip to main content" pill drops in at the top. Put .pc-skip-link first in the body.

              • Contrast

                Text reaches 7:1 in Paper and Dusk, and npm run check fails the build if a change breaks that.

              • High contrast

                In Windows Contrast themes, pills, tags and cards get real borders, and selected things use the system highlight.

              • Keyboard and motion

                Every control has a visible focus ring and a 44px target. Tabs and menus follow the arrow-key patterns. Reduced motion stops the wobble.

              Using it

              Plain CSS classes and custom properties, so any framework can use them as they are.
